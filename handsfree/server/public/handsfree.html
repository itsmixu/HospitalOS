Connecting this page to the local ward…

Phone and glasses setup
  1. Connect the Android phone to this computer by USB. Wear the glasses and check that Meta AI shows them connected.
  2. In HospitalOS on the phone: allow Bluetooth, connect to Meta AI, then start the glasses display. Meta AI Developer Mode must be enabled.
  3. Keep HospitalOS open and the phone unlocked. This page connects Aino’s display and Ben’s demo responder automatically.
  4. For speech, allow the glasses microphone in phone setup, then tap Enable glasses voice. Say “Hey Lumi”, pause for Listening, then your command. Glasses voice stays enabled until stopped, the phone app leaves the foreground, or the glasses disconnect. The display stays clear between interactions; new requests and messages bring it back.

Reconnect demo reconnects this web page. It cannot pair Bluetooth, grant phone permissions, or restart a failed glasses session.

Bedside reading demo and saved records

Open the blood-pressure monitor

Use the supported synthetic HOS-BP-1 monitor scene. Camera access must be allowed in phone setup.

  1. “Hey Lumi” → “Select patient demo A” → review → tilt right to confirm.
  2. “Capture reading” → look at the supported monitor.
  3. Review every value → tilt right to save. To correct: “Correct systolic 141”.
  4. “Share reading with Ben” → review → tilt right to send.

Pause for each new card. Say “Hey Lumi” again if the command window has closed. Saved records are synthetic local records, not an EHR. Captured time does not prove measurement freshness.
